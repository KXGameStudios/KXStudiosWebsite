The game is still in development, so some details on this page may change before release.

## Goal

A bout is won by forcing your opponent out of the ring, or by making them touch the clay with anything other than their feet.

## Ways to play

- **Career.** Create one wrestler and guide them from their first tournament to retirement.
- **Viewer.** Found a world and watch it run. Everything in the world is open to look at.
- **Test Battle.** Pick any two wrestlers and fight. Results here do not count toward anything.

## Tournaments and ranks

There are six tournaments a year, each 15 days long. Wrestlers in the top two divisions fight every day. Wrestlers in the four lower divisions fight seven bouts.

After each tournament the rankings are redrawn. More wins than losses moves a wrestler up, and more losses than wins moves them down. The best record in a division wins its title.

## Fighting a bout

Every bout can be simulated, watched or fought by hand. When you fight:

- **The charge.** Wait for the referee's signal, then tap or flick forward. A fast reaction gives a stronger charge. Charging before the signal is a false start.
- **Moving.** Hold and drag to move toward, away from or around your opponent.
- **Thrust.** Tap, or flick toward your opponent.
- **Pull.** Flick back. This punishes an opponent who has pushed too hard.
- **Sidestep.** Flick sideways.
- **Grab.** Takes hold of the belt. With a grip, flick toward your opponent to drive them back, or press Throw or Trip.
- **Brace.** Hold to resist a push.

Each move beats one move and loses to another. A thrust beats a grab, a grab beats a sidestep, and a sidestep beats a thrust.

Backing away for too long drains your fighting spirit, and the referee will call you back in.

## Common questions

**Do I have to fight the bouts myself?**
No. Any bout can be simulated or watched instead.

**Is my progress saved?**
The game saves on its own after every tournament and when you leave the app. You can also save by hand.
