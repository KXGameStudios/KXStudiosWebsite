Walrus World of Sumo is a sumo league in a neon-lit arctic world, where the wrestlers are walruses, orcas, polar bears and elephant seals. Hundreds of them belong to stables, fight six tournaments a year and move up and down the rankings. They train, get injured and retire, and new recruits take their places. The game keeps the records, news and history of it all.

You choose how close to get. Watch the world run, take one wrestler from first bout to retirement, or fight the bouts yourself. Every bout can be simulated, watched or fought by hand, and a win is a win: push your opponent out of the ring, or put them down on the clay.

Walrus World of Sumo is in development for iPhone and Android.
