This policy explains what information Blocks of the Ides ("the game") collects, why, and what you can do about it. The game is made by KX Game Studios, a partnership consisting of Aleksander Schultz and Alexander Wallace.

## The short version

- The game has no accounts. It never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.
- Your progress, settings and hints are saved on your device, not on our servers. We do not run any servers for the game.
- The game shows ads and measures how it is played, using the outside services listed below. Those services collect some information about your device.

## What is stored on your device

Your level progress, daily puzzle results and streak, hint balance, settings, purchases and consent choices are saved on your device. If your phone's own backup is switched on, this data is included in that backup. Deleting the game deletes it from the device.

## What outside services collect

**Ads: Google AdMob.** The game shows ads between some levels, and offers optional ads in exchange for hints and other rewards. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, general location worked out from the IP address, device and app details, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before any ad is requested or loaded. On iPhone and iPad the game also shows Apple's tracking permission prompt. Buying Remove Ads stops the ads between levels; the optional reward ads remain available.

**Analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase.** We use this to see how the game is played (which levels are finished, how often hints are used, which settings are popular, etc). It collects gameplay events, your device model, operating system, app version, language and country, and an anonymous ID created for your copy of the game. We do not send names, email addresses, advertising IDs, precise location, or anything you type. Where consent is required, analytics stays off until you have answered the consent prompt, and stays off if you decline.

**Crash reports: Sentry.** If the game crashes or hits an error, a report may be sent with the error details, app version, operating system and device model, so that we can fix it.

**Purchases: Apple App Store and Google Play.** Purchases are handled by the store you downloaded the game from. We never see your card or payment details. The game keeps a record of completed purchases on your device so that it can unlock what you bought.

These services handle information under their own policies:

- [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Sentry privacy policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/)
- [Apple privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## Notifications and sharing

If you switch on the daily puzzle reminder, the notification is scheduled on your own device. Nothing is sent to us.

Share Result opens your phone's share screen with a short text about your puzzle result. You choose whether and where to send it.

## Your choices

- **Ad and analytics consent.** In regions where consent is required, you can change your answer at any time under Settings, then Privacy options, in the game.
- **Tracking on iPhone and iPad.** You can change the permission in the device's Settings, under Privacy and Security, then Tracking.
- **Advertising ID.** You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.
- **Reminders.** You can switch the daily reminder off in the game's Settings.
- **Everything else.** Deleting the game removes all data stored on your device.

## Children

The game is appropriate for audiences of all ages and is not directed at any particular age demographic. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete the game. Analytics data is kept by Google for [retention period set in the Firebase console]. Crash reports are kept by Sentry for [retention period set in the Sentry project].

Because the game has no accounts, we cannot look up data by your name or email address. If you have a question about your data, contact us and we will help where we can.
