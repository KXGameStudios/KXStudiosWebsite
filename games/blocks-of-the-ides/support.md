## Goal

Place the pieces on the board so that every open space is filled.

## Controls

- Drag a piece from the tray onto the board. A green outline shows where it will land.
- Tap a piece in the tray to select it, then use the rotate buttons to turn it or the flip button to mirror it.
- Tap a piece on the board, then press delete to send it back to the tray.
- Reset clears every piece from the board.

## Hints

A hint shows where one piece can go. The marker stays until the board changes. If the pieces you have placed cannot lead to a solution, the hint tells you to reset instead. Each hint costs one, either way.

You get hints at the start, when you open a new world, by watching an ad, or in the shop.

## Levels

Finish level 20 to complete a world. That unlocks the next world, and some worlds also unlock a game mode. Levels 21 to 30 are bonus levels: harder, and never needed to unlock anything.

## Later worlds

- **Pinned pieces (Pantheon).** A piece with a pin cannot be rotated or flipped. Place it just as it lies in the tray.
- **Walls (Aqueduct).** A piece cannot sit across a wall. Every space still has to be filled.
- **Spare pieces (Senate).** There are more pieces than the board needs. Fill the board and the level is done, whatever is left in the tray.

## Game modes

- **Daily Puzzle.** One new board every day. Finish it to keep your streak going. Unlocks when you finish the tutorial.
- **Time Trial.** Solve as many puzzles as you can before the clock runs out. Each solve adds time. Unlocks when you complete Colosseum.
- **Nightmare.** The hardest puzzles. If you are stuck, the skip button moves you on. Unlocks when you complete Pantheon.

## Common questions

**I bought something and it is missing.**
Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases. This brings back Remove Ads, theme packs and the Daily Archive.

**Do my hints move to a new phone?**
Hints are stored on your device. They carry over only if your phone's own backup is switched on and restored. Restore Purchases does not bring back hints.

**How do I turn off the daily reminder?**
Open Settings in the game and switch the reminder off.

**How do I change my ad privacy choices?**
Open Settings in the game and tap Privacy options. The entry appears only in regions where a choice is required.
