Each level gives you a wooden board and a tray of pieces. Drag the pieces onto the board, turning and flipping them as you go, until every open space is filled.

Early levels take a minute or two, so a puzzle fits into a short break. The boards get bigger and the rules get trickier as you move through the worlds.

Blocks of the Ides is coming to iPhone and Android, in English, Spanish, French and Portuguese. Two extra looks, Cyberpunk and Vaporwave, can be bought in the shop if you want a change from the wood.
